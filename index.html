// Why we started NINE

We believe you don't have to be a “golfer” to play golf.

There's a picture of who a golfer is supposed to be, and plenty of people who love the driving range don't think it's them. So they never take the next step.

We think that picture is wrong. You don't need a perfect swing, the right clothes or to know every rule to belong on a golf course. Golf is mostly a few hours outside with people you like, being present. NINE exists to get you there.

Harry and Dan, co-founders

Quick questions

Do I need my own clubs?

No. Most driving ranges lend or hire out clubs.

What else will I pay for?

Just your range balls, and a green fee when you play your 9 holes.

What if a session goes badly?

Reply REPEAT and go again. Rough sessions are part of it.
